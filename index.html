Venue notes

Before the first swing.

Will my space work?

Measure clear width, depth and ceiling height, including light fixtures. Note power access, load-in distance and a spectator area outside the swing zone. Final fit depends on the equipment and venue review.

What if we plan to play outside?

Identify a level setup area with power and an indoor backup. Include weather and daylight in the venue walkthrough.

How do we keep guests moving?

Use three-shot challenges at larger events. Reserve full holes and course play for smaller groups with more time per player.

Which towns can I plan for?

Start with Erie, Boulder, Lafayette, Broomfield, Longmont and the Northern Colorado Front Range. Travel and availability would be confirmed with the event quote.
